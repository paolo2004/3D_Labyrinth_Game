Game Description

Explore the 3D labyrinth and find the win coin, Little Pony. Collecting Little Pony wins the game.

Items And Points

  • Key: 1 point
  • Double key: 2 points
  • Coin: 3 points

Lives And Traps

You start with 2 lives. Every 6 points gives you 1 extra life. If you touch a trap, you lose 1 life. If you lose all lives, the game is over.
