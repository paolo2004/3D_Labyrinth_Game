How To Play

Every level is a fixed labyrinth, so you can learn your way around it. Collect every key to unlock the exit, then reach the win coin, Little Pony, in the far corner. Touching the exit before you hold all keys leaves it locked, so come back once you have them.

Items And Points

  • Key — 1 point, and one of the keys you need
  • Double key — 2 points, bonus only
  • Coin — 3 points, bonus only

Items and traps are placed anew every round, so the same labyrinth never plays the same way twice.

Lives And Traps

Points earn extra lives. Touching a trap — the glowing red square on the floor — costs one life, and the trap then disappears. At zero lives the game is over.
